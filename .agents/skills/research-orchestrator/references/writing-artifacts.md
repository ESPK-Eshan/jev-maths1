## Evidence-to-outline contract

The author receives selected route/type/venue, frozen protocol and result/source manifests, supported and unsupported claim register, important negative findings, scoped literature/gap matrix, actual author inputs, deliverable inventory and allowed edits. Output a section-to-claim/evidence outline, contribution/limitation framing and figure/table plan before full prose. A manuscript-only import exposes unverified experimental/novelty assumptions rather than certifying them by rewriting.

Draft methods and evidence-based results before finalizing introduction, abstract, title and broad claims. Explain actual question, bounded contribution, relevant related work, problem formulation, method/equations/pseudocode, reproducible setup, baselines/metrics/statistics/resources, results/ablations, interpretation, limitations and conclusion. Add declarations, references and supplements required by the actual article type. Adapt the structure to method, comparison, systems, analysis, negative/replication, theory or review evidence; do not fill sections with invented experiments. An abstract states achieved evidence and caveats. Clear scholarly prose uses concrete motivation, paragraph progression, precise mechanisms and consistent terminology without generic filler or invented experiences.

## Reproducible tables and measured figures

Choose displays to answer actual questions: useful main comparisons, compute/search-cost accounting, ablations, quality–cost or latency tradeoffs, robustness/uncertainty, failure/example analysis and method diagram. No mandatory chart count. Related-work tables distinguish measured, author-reported and unavailable facts and avoid ranking incomparable protocols.

Generate every measured table/plot from stored analysis outputs. Its manifest records figure/table ID, supported claim IDs, raw/result IDs and hashes, aggregation/transformation/exclusion rules, units, independent sample count, uncertainty meaning, generation script/environment, output hash and caption. ## Claim and citation audit

Bind factual/numeric claims to experiment/analysis receipts or verified primary sources with useful page/section/figure locators. Retrieve bibliographic identity from real records and confirm version/publication status; never construct references from memory. DOI existence alone does not support a proposition, and search snippets/generated summaries are discovery aids. Fetch Consensus records before citation and inspect actual primary content for substantive claims. Unavailable support stays visibly unresolved in a draft and cannot silently enter fully verified export.

Material source/citation/equation/prose changes trigger affected rechecks and current manuscript hashes.
