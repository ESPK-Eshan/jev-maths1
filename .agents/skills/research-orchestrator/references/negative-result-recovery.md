# Negative and inconclusive result recovery

Read after a valid closed pilot/development/confirmation finding challenges the hypothesis, including harmful tradeoffs and imprecise evidence. This is phase-specific stage 14 analysis and stage 15 decision, not an added controller or a demand to find positive results. A pilot reaches this branch before optional development or confirmation; see [pipeline](pipeline.md).

## Diagnose before discovering

The analysis agent receives frozen protocol, raw outcomes and hashes, failures/denominators, original claims, selection history and permitted closed evidence. Distinguish:

| Finding | Output and next action |
|---|---|
| Invalid instrumentation/evaluator, leakage or broken method | Defect evidence and affected dependencies; repair/reverify before scientific interpretation. No discovery claim from invalid results. |
| Disconfirmed prediction, harm or informative boundary | Original planned contrast, effect/uncertainty/practical relevance and rivals; assess a faithful negative/replication/analysis route. |
| Valid but imprecise result | Precision and missing information, plausible effects still compatible with data; affordable discriminating follow-up or inconclusive scope. Non-significance is not equivalence. |
| Missing/unknown outcome | Frozen failure/missingness disposition and operational reconciliation; no invented neutral measurement. |

Preserve original hypothesis, primary and prespecified secondary analyses, uncertainty, adverse outcomes, failed tasks, denominators, selection and full compute cost. Inspect quality–cost tradeoffs, robustness, calibration, error clusters and heterogeneous responses only where available data support them. Separate seed variation from independent task/population variation and account for dependent units. Correlation alone does not establish mechanism; sparse or unstable subgroup patterns are limitations.

Use relevant Orchestra evaluation and ARS research contracts first, with [statistical analysis](C:/Users/eshan/.agents/skills/statistical-analysis/SKILL.md) and [critical thinking](C:/Users/eshan/.agents/skills/scientific-critical-thinking/SKILL.md) as complementary checks. Contextual effect/uncertainty reasoning follows the principles in the [ASA statement](https://www.amstat.org/asa/files/pdfs/p-valuestatement.pdf), not significance-only rescue.

## Bounded exploratory candidate inventory

Allocate exploration effort/candidate count before additional searching. Each recovery card records timestamp, explicit post-hoc/exploratory status, original data/analysis locators and hashes, examined pattern/contrast, sample counts and independent unit, search/selection procedure, practical effect, mechanism, serious rivals, falsifier, cheapest discriminating follow-up, costs and inference limits. Retain every examined candidate/pattern, including unsuccessful and declined ones; do not delete result-dependent history.

A genuinely prespecified secondary analysis keeps its status and multiplicity obligations. A selected post-hoc pattern cannot inherit those privileges. Ordinary intervals/p-values after adaptive selection are not automatically confirmatory; use justified selection-aware methods when available or make transparently descriptive claims. Generic multiplicity correction cannot repair undisclosed searching. Stop exploration at its bound rather than search until significant.

The novelty skeptic performs an ARS/Orchestra counter-search against published work, current preprints, alternate terminology and closest explanations. It records overlap, source access limits, rivals and falsifiers. A filled recovery card is not proof that the new explanation is true or novel.

## Choose an evidence-supported route

The coordinator compares faithful negative/replication paper, failure-mode/efficiency analysis, narrowed claim, new hypothesis needing follow-up, and honest inconclusive report. Record evidence strength, importance, rivals, novelty boundary, feasible fresh confirmation, remaining resources, chosen/declined routes and next obligations. Preserve original negative results even if a later hypothesis succeeds. No route guarantees publication.

The existing fresh verifier and critic assess both diagnosis and decision under the unchanged frozen-rubric, at-least-five criteria, mean-90, maximum-two-reloop rules. Valid unfavorable outcomes can pass stage quality. Scientific blockers remain nonwaivable. A recovery pivot is not a hidden third repair round; original stage lineages and separate project pivot limits remain binding.

## Fresh-evidence follow-up boundary

After original confirmation closes and its planned analysis is recorded, those outputs may inspire new exploratory hypotheses. Once viewed, they are discovery data for those hypotheses. Before seeing suitable fresh outcomes, freeze a hypothesis-specific follow-up protocol. Appropriate evidence may be a genuinely sealed unused partition with justified independent units, additional independent tasks/data or an independent replication.

Reshuffling viewed data, calling a post-hoc split untouched, or adding seeds on the selected tasks cannot establish independent task-level confirmation. Extra seeds can answer a limited seed-variability question. [Preregistration guidance](https://www.cos.io/initiatives/prereg) supports separating generation and testing; external registration is optional unless required and no retrospective registration becomes prospective.

If several recovery hypotheses get tests, freeze the confirmatory family, selection/stopping policy and suitable multiplicity/sequential treatment before their outcomes. Record every consequential follow-up, including failure and inconclusive evidence; fresh data do not permit winner-only reporting. A further data-inspired change is another disclosed exploratory decision with its own evidence and budget obligations.

A chosen follow-up reenters stages 7–9, immediately refreshes stage 11 resource recommendations, then uses implementation/pilot, optional development, new frozen confirmation, analysis and decision under linked protocol history. Reconcile old campaign/Goal/remote jobs first; never retarget a live fixed controller metric or verifier. Reserve confirmation and closeout funds before new development. If fresh validation is unavailable or unjustified, keep the hypothesis exploratory and complete a defensible scoped report when its obligations pass.

## Persist and hand off

Store `.research/recovery/` evidence outside editable experiment scope: diagnosis, complete pattern/candidate inventory, chosen/declined routes, protocol/claim/review/budget links, access history, inference limits, stopping rationale and next permitted action. Reused experiments retain original IDs/costs rather than count twice. [OKF handoffs](knowledge-bundle.md) expose only permitted evidence; comprehensive logs cannot leak sealed confirmation. Methods/results/discussion distinguish original predictions, exploratory discovery and actually confirmed follow-up. Route status keeps unrun development/confirmation accurately pending, stopped or verified not applicable.
