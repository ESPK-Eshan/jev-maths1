## Investment levels and stage outputs

| Level | Required output | Gate and next action |
|---|---|---|
| Instrumentation smoke | Minimal actual data-to-metric execution, logging/leakage/control/expected-failure checks | Engineering evidence only. Repair invalid measurement before scientific use. |
| Scientific pilot | Credible baseline and smallest prespecified discriminating test, raw outcomes and scope limits | Stage 12(pilot) → 14(pilot-analysis) → 15(pilot-decision). Promising evidence may enable development or direct freeze; valid negative/inconclusive evidence enters recovery. |
| Development | Bounded coherent hypotheses, fixed development objective/guards, complete trial history and search cost | Optional stage 13; select candidate under the frozen rule. Development is exploratory and cannot consume sealed confirmation evidence. |
| Confirmation | Frozen candidate/protocol, untouched appropriate evidence, actual outcomes and planned analysis | Separate stage 12(confirmation) → 14(final-analysis) → 15(final-decision); adequate inference for the scoped claim or honest inconclusive result. |

Cheap routes may use frozen small models, cached intermediates, targeted data, retrieval/inference changes or parameter-efficient adaptation when scientifically appropriate. Record approximation/representativeness limits; affordability cannot silently redefine the question. Large sweeps/pretraining need scientific justification and actual authorization. Immediate post-design hardware recommendations and later measured updates follow stage 11, before full implementation; an OOM/precision/data-path change cannot silently amend the method.

## Freeze a valid scientific protocol

Before target outcomes are viewed, persist question/hypothesis and rivals; target population and independent unit; candidate/baselines and comparable tuning/search budgets; immutable code/data/model/tokenizer/retrieval/judge/environment IDs where available; splits and access controls; primary/secondary outcomes/contrasts; meaningful effect/precision target; allocation/seeds; metric validity; guards; exclusions/failure/retry/missingness rules; aggregation/uncertainty; multiplicity/interim/stopping rules; planned displays; smoke/pilot/development/confirmation allocations; and bounded reproduction tolerances. Protocol amendments preserve prior versions and prospectivity status.

Measurement validity is a scientific requirement. Validate automatic/model judges against a justified reference or existing validation; retain model/version, prompt/decoding, candidate ordering/blinding, calibration and position/preference bias/error evidence. Check that retrieval, diffusion or agent metrics measure the stated property and do not reward leakage or a trivial artifact. Repeatability alone is insufficient. Missing essential validity blocks the claim.

Use appropriate paired contrasts when justified. Distinguish example-sampling uncertainty from training-seed variation; repeated checkpoints/generated examples are not independent model replications. No fixed seed count guarantees precision. Handle primary confirmatory families, tasks/subgroups and repeated interim looks by prespecified suitable error control or explicitly descriptive inference. Non-significance is not equivalence. Best-seed reporting, dropped unfavorable tasks and significance-based stopping violate the protocol.

Pin actual immutable revisions/content hashes when available. Mutable model/API aliases remain mutable; record missing provider fingerprints, drift checks and any required amendment/reevaluation. Identical seeds do not ensure bitwise equality across hardware/libraries. Record benchmark pretraining exposure separately from this project's holdout access; unknown contamination stays unknown. Selected domain details include RAG corpus/chunk/index and question-answer provenance; agent tool/environment/reset/shared-state/budget versions; diffusion sampler/noise/reference population and metric sample protocol. See [PyTorch reproducibility](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/notes/randomness.html) and [dataset revisions](https://huggingface.co/docs/datasets/loading) for relevant source guidance, rechecked when operational details matter.

On this Windows setup, foreground is the initial supported path. Before invoking controller status/archive/resume on an existing run, inspect immutable mode and runtime-file presence without PID probes. Background/unknown modes remain excluded until compatibility is actually validated. Do not call detached liveness paths merely to discover status. Apply the [integration preflight](integrations.md) before the workflow's ordinary status command. This is a local compatibility constraint, not a general claim that upstream background mode never works.

Check the current thread's Goal namespace for a conflict before initialization. Initialize successfully before creating a matching Goal; if baseline already meets target, do not create one. Goal creation requires explicit user/system authorization; a research request alone does not supply it. One foreground campaign at a time in this coordinator's actual namespace; separate repositories do not remove a conflicting unfinished Goal. Do not invent an account-wide restriction or create extra chats to bypass ownership.

Each coherent trial changes only approved scope and ends through the documented `finish` command. Use its measured receipts and keep/discard/status results. Wrappers cannot change metric, guard or exit semantics. A local timeout does not prove a remote job ended; reconcile durable ownership before retries/next trials and never attribute late results to a new candidate. ## Trial accounting and stopping

Classify measured candidate/task failure, evaluator/infrastructure nonmeasurement, and incomplete/unknown outcome separately. An evaluator-defined timeout, failed episode, invalid action or resource violation can be a valid unfavorable outcome and stays in the prespecified denominator. Broken measurement is not a scientific null. Preserve total attempts, valid tests, guard outcomes, missingness, costs and failed shards. Never retry until success and retain only the success.

Agree a minimum useful exploration target and maximum allocation; minimum is never authority to exceed the maximum. Optional planning defaults are three valid pilot trials after baseline/six total attempts, then six valid development trials/twelve attempts with a four-valid-trial plateau window. Use only if suitable and accepted in the project contract; they are not universal requirements or overrides of user bounds.

Define practical improvement and repeat-measurement noise. A plateau requires a window without meaningful gain after adequate affordable exploration plus inspection of trial diversity; cosmetic parameter tweaks do not establish useful exploration. Propose a materially different literature/error-backed strategy or prefer an ablation, stronger baseline, mechanism test or confirmation. Continue only to resolve a concrete uncertainty inside the contract.

Target reached ends that campaign and enables scientific assessment/confirmation; it does not prove novelty or paper validity. Stop at prespecified futility, exhausted limits, invalid measurement, unrecoverable state, user stop or defeated premise. An unmet target cannot be marked Goal-complete or artificially blocked to free a namespace. Follow actual pause/block conditions. For foreground strategy changes, finish/reconcile the coherent trial, cease dispatch and resolve the matching Goal with authorized native controls before archiving/new campaign; background-only CLI `stop` is not the solution. If required pause/clear control is user-only/unavailable, report that exact prerequisite. Never silently change an active metric/target/verify command. Preserve original costs and state.

## Confirmation and selected special study types

Keep final cases, outputs and error analyses sealed from optimizers until selection and evaluation close. Record access history; prompt discipline alone is not enforced isolation. Use filesystem/process restrictions when required and disclose actual limitations. A fresh `confirmation_executor` receives only the frozen protocol and approved resources; it does not tune or relay holdout examples mid-run. Repair needs a recorded amendment/reverification. If a repair changes method/statistical contract, reassess whether untouched evidence remains. Final analysis consumes outputs only after close. Viewed holdout evidence requires new suitable evidence or explicitly exploratory framing, not relabeling.

Actually executed simulator studies may support simulator-scoped claims. Retain environment/version/config, task/initial-state distribution, dynamics, rewards, termination/truncation, seeds, policy/model state, raw trajectories and aggregation receipts. Label synthetic provenance and generator assumptions. Placeholder/invented framework results are not simulator studies; simulator evidence alone is not real-world deployment evidence. Follow actual backend semantics such as the [Gymnasium API](https://gymnasium.farama.org/api/env/) when selected.

Offline policy/value claims require an identifiable estimand/discount/horizon, behavior propensity availability (logged versus estimated), state/action support, trajectory-level units, reward/observability/confounding assumptions, estimator validity, uncertainty and sensitivity. Use estimator-appropriate separation/cross-fitting and support/weight instability/extrapolation diagnostics where needed. Unknown propensities do not universally bar every estimator, but each method must justify its own identification. A favorable finite estimate alone cannot pass. If unsupported, narrow to descriptive/predictive claims, a justified simulator study or bounded decision-quality question. [Thomas–Brunskill](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/thomasa16.pdf) is an example of method-specific conditions, not a prescribed universal estimator.

At every phase, output the closed evidence bundle, original and achieved protocol scope, complete costs/failures, supported claims/limitations, current verifier/critic receipts and the next permitted action. Valid negative/inconclusive outcomes follow [recovery](negative-result-recovery.md); the two critic reloops are independent of trial/pivot budgets.
