# Original writing and authorized report review

Read when drafting, editing source-dependent passages or interpreting a supplied similarity/AI-writing report. ARS source/integrity/review contracts lead; Orchestra ML/systems writing supplies technical argument and complementary scientific-writing checks fill needs. See [writing artifacts](writing-artifacts.md) and exact [integrations](integrations.md). Natural prose is a writing-quality goal, not evidence of human-only authorship or detector evasion.

## Evidence-first writing and attribution

Build a claim-to-evidence outline from verified results, literature and original project interpretation. Write from that outline rather than editing copied paragraphs. Attach citations to supported propositions, use precise methods and qualified conclusions, and follow supplied author preferences without inventing experiences or contributions.

Maintain an attribution register with passage/figure/table ID, source and locator, source-dependent claim, necessary quotation, reuse from prior author work, adapted diagram/table or method/data description, license/permission, citation/quotation status and review disposition. Distinguish ordinary terminology/journal boilerplate from close paraphrase, unattributed copying, incorrect quotation/source meaning and undisclosed reuse.

For problematic overlap, return to primary evidence and express the point within this project's own argument, retain accurate attribution, or quote where necessary/permitted. A citation does not justify unmarked copied wording. Removing citations, invisible characters, synonym spinning or corrupting technical language to reduce a score is not a repair. Recheck meaning, equations, numbers, scope and source support after material edits; update manuscript hashes and affected integrity/claim/review receipts.

The goal is eliminate unattributed copying and needless borrowed expression, not meet an invented universal percentage. Follow actual documented institution/venue policy. Local overlap checks disclose comparison-corpus limits and cannot be called a Turnitin database check or proof that plagiarism is absent. Legitimate quotations, technical terms and references may match. [Turnitin similarity guidance](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/23435833938701-Understanding-the-similarity-score) explains why a match score requires interpretation rather than deciding misconduct itself; recheck current institutional rules when applying them.

## Version-bound report intake

No checker connector, institutional account, upload or completed report is assumed. Review a user-supplied report or an actually configured authorized service. Before externally uploading an unpublished manuscript, resolve the actual account/data-storage/repository authority, including later self-match implications. Standing Drive backup authority does not authorize an unrelated checker upload.

Preserve the unmodified submitted draft and original report under `.research/originality/`. Bind report/submission ID when exposed, exact submitted draft hash, timestamp, service/version if exposed, processing/result status, documented settings/exclusions, comparison scope and actual institutional policy. Missing fields stay unknown. Failed, unavailable, processing or untested reports are never reported as zero. Different settings or manuscript versions must remain visible rather than compared as equivalent tests.

For each substantive match, record passage/source locators, classification, citation/quotation/permission status, corrective action or evidence-backed legitimacy, owner, affected claim and verification disposition. Prioritize attribution, close copying, wrong quotes, permissions and self-reuse. Preserve old/new drafts and every report. Disclose any legitimate institution-approved exclusions; do not change filters to hide copying or manufacture a low total. Low overall similarity cannot close a known integrity fault; high similarity alone does not prove misconduct.

## AI indicator and author review

Keep AI-writing indicators separate from similarity, scientific originality and native critic scores. [Turnitin's AI-report guidance](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/22774058814093-Using-the-AI-Writing-Report) describes an independent indicator that can misidentify writing. Inspect passages for quality, author understanding and provenance. Do not guarantee a detector pass, optimize loops for evasion, conceal assistance or manufacture human-only history. Preserve actual assistance records and apply the real disclosure policy. Apparent false positives go to human institutional review with process evidence; an AI cannot certify them as false.

Deliver the readable manuscript, attribution/match register, relevant version-bound reports, unresolved restrictions and revision summary. Required human approval, authorship/contribution/funding/declaration inputs are recorded only when supplied. An unresolved copying, fabricated citation or invented author declaration is a blocker regardless of critic mean or detector output.

Output states may include evidence-audited draft, originality-review-pending, originality-reviewed-with-stated-coverage, journal-format-checked draft, author-review-pending and author-approved submission package. Report only achieved states. A coverage-limited local check, polished prose or low score cannot confer final approval. Material repairs return through current source/integrity/review gates under the bounded [reloop contract](journal-stage-critique.md).
