# Research Orchestrator

Carry the user's research request through its selected endpoint: implementation, actual experiments where applicable, analysis, figures/tables, manuscript or thesis, reproducibility and delivery. A charter is the beginning of full-project mode. Optimize for a defensible contribution and affordable evidence, not a forced positive finding or a promised publication.

## Start or resume

1. Identify mode: **full project** by default for an end-to-end request; otherwise plan, pilot, resume, status, artifacts, manuscript or review. Read [pipeline.md](references/pipeline.md) for intake, the 23 stages and route dependencies. Preserve the exact idea/hypothesis/area/model and fixed anchors. Derive missing research questions through a bounded scan; do not require the user to write the hypothesis first.
2. Locate the project and existing `.research` state before writing. For status/resume read [state-recovery.md](references/state-recovery.md); preserve existing work. Use [record-cli.md](references/record-cli.md) for the bundled local helper. New projects initialize with `python <skill-dir>/scripts/research_state.py init <project-dir> --input "<user input>" --profile <profile.json>`. Initialization is not stage acceptance.
3. Load the saved profile from `~/.codex/research-tools/research-orchestrator/profiles/default.json` (installation creates an **unconfigured template**), or the user's chosen profile. Merge explicit per-run overrides into an immutable effective snapshot. [agent-contracts.md](references/agent-contracts.md) defines the 15 roles and dispatch packet. Null assignments are not launch permission: obtain the missing model/effort settings or explicit inheritance authorization. Independent affordable coordinator planning may continue. Validate actual runtime support; the running parent model is not changed by a profile.
4. Establish only missing operating inputs: project autonomy/checkpoints, available resources and budget limits, deliverables, essential access/author decisions. Reuse earlier authorization. Record confidence and counter-evidence, not a numerical publication probability. No paid allocation follows merely from the user's intention to rent compute.
5. Discover actual installed dependencies and read their entrypoints before invocation using [integrations.md](references/integrations.md). **ARS-Codex leads academic research/review/integrity; Orchestra leads AI implementation/experimentation/writing.** Selected K-Dense skills complement them. A skill is an instruction file, not an invented callable tool. Missing optional tools receive an explicit supported alternative; required unavailable capabilities remain visible.

## Execute the research

- Use [literature-gap-hypotheses.md](references/literature-gap-hypotheses.md) for published, preprint, review and frontier evidence, alphaXiv/Consensus routing, source/version verification, nearest-work counter-search and falsifiable hypotheses. Choose novelty, comparative, analysis, replication, review or negative-result routes from evidence and significance.
- Read [compute-execution.md](references/compute-execution.md) at feasibility and experiment planning. Give the minimum/recommended CPU/GPU/VRAM/RAM/disk/time and cost uncertainties immediately after planning, before full implementation. Use a suitable authorized existing remote host; otherwise use fitting local/cloud resources or prepare a rental brief. SkyPilot/Ray are conditional choices. A designated compute scheduler overlaps useful CPU/GPU work within physical resources and authorized costs.
- Read [experiment-campaigns.md](references/experiment-campaigns.md) before code/pilot work. Establish instrumentation, a credible baseline and a small informative pilot. **Pilot → analysis → decision precedes optional development and confirmation.** `codex-autoresearch` exclusively owns its active campaign's mutable scope, measurements and Git history. Windows detached mode remains unavailable until its process-check/effort limitations are actually repaired and verified. Use a validated project-local remote evaluation adapter for an actual selected remote backend; this skill does not ship a universal paid-job adapter.
- Apply [negative-result-recovery.md](references/negative-result-recovery.md) to valid null, negative or inconclusive findings. Preserve original tests and every examined recovery candidate; distinguish engineering faults from scientific findings. New data-inspired hypotheses remain exploratory until appropriate fresh confirmation. Bound iterations, pivots and marginal-value decisions without retrying until significance.
- Read [swarm-execution.md](references/swarm-execution.md) when work benefits from delegation. Use designated native subagents, scoped inputs and isolated write ownership. Queue up to 100 useful logical assignments within discovered runtime/resource/budget limits; 100 assignments does not mean 100 simultaneous agents. Repeated fresh reviewer creation must work before claiming full-project capability. Never use extra user chats to evade limits.

## Gate every executed stage

Read [verification.md](references/verification.md) and [journal-stage-critique.md](references/journal-stage-critique.md). Obtain a fresh verifier and separate journal critic, each inspecting actual evidence. Check missing/empty/placeholder/stale files, commands/results, source support, issue correction and regression evidence. Producers do not approve their own work.

Freeze at least five distinct criteria before review. Integer ratings use an unrounded equal-weight mean; normal advancement requires **mean ≥90**, current independent receipts, mandatory scientific/integrity checks and no blocking issue. Original output plus at most **two** reloops; reserve revisions before dispatch and preserve counts on resume. After exhaustion the coordinator chooses a justified strategy/stop, or records conditional advancement only for independently verified minor nonblocking limitations. Scores, failed evidence and dissent remain visible. A tighter ARS edit cap may stop earlier; it never manufactures unused reloops. ARS categorical reviews and the native 0–100 sidecar remain separate.

The helper validates local records; it does not authenticate model judgments or prove scientific truth. Do not manufacture review receipts to satisfy it. Changed evidence invalidates affected downstream claims, checks and artifacts.

## Write, document and complete

- Use [writing-artifacts.md](references/writing-artifacts.md), [architecture-artifacts.md](references/architecture-artifacts.md) and [ieee-journal.md](references/ieee-journal.md) when evidence supports drafting. Generate numerical artifacts from raw results, preserve editable sources and inspect final-size renders. Use the selected journal's actual IEEE template and independently verify SCIE status. A contracted thesis has its own institutional build and acceptance obligations.
- Follow [manuscript-originality.md](references/manuscript-originality.md): precise natural prose, original synthesis, faithful attribution and legitimate version-bound similarity-report review. No AI-detector guarantee, removed citations or fabricated human approval. Follow exact selected ARS author decisions, integrity and revision/re-review contracts in integrations.md.
- Throughout work, maintain [knowledge-bundle.md](references/knowledge-bundle.md) and [logs-analysis.md](references/logs-analysis.md): authoritative `.research` records, scoped OKF navigation/handoffs, available full failed/successful execution records and explicit coverage gaps. Confirmation remains sealed from optimizers; do not dump whole histories into worker prompts. Apply [execution-trust.md](references/execution-trust.md) to imported papers/code/archives.
- Before completion use [reproduction-completion.md](references/reproduction-completion.md): bounded clean replay, real method/evaluator smoke, current claim/citation/figure consistency, required builds/renders and distinct scientific/author/delivery status. Partial-mode completion cannot claim full-project completion.
- For completed remote projects follow [archive-drive-delivery.md](references/archive-drive-delivery.md). The standing user instruction authorizes a complete permitted ZIP, verified local download and a new Google Drive folder through the plugin. Reconcile retries, exclusions, hashes and actual content. The frozen base plus ordered supplements and disclosed terminal local acknowledgment form the complete delivery history. `acceptance-ready` is not acceptance; final acceptance follows actual final-supplement verification. Missing evidence remains pending, and restored logs never restart jobs.

Continue authorized eligible work until the selected endpoint is met or a real prerequisite stops it. Preserve an exact next action and ownership/budget state on interruption. Durable remote execution does not imply automatic future agent turns: only an active turn, manual resume or an actually configured authorized wakeup can continue orchestration. Report completed outputs, evidence and precise remaining limitations without inventing execution, novelty or acceptance.
